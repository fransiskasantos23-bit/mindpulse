# 🧠 MindPulse

### Empowering Emotional Wellbeing Through Self-Awareness

MindPulse is a modern web application designed to help users understand, monitor, and improve their emotional wellbeing. Through daily mood tracking, stress assessment, and guided wellness exercises, MindPulse transforms emotional self-care into a simple and engaging experience.

---

## 🌟 Overview

Mental and emotional wellbeing are essential parts of a healthy life. MindPulse was created to provide individuals with accessible tools for emotional reflection, stress management, and personal growth.

The platform enables users to track their emotional states, identify patterns, and practice healthy coping strategies through a clean and intuitive interface.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Mood Tracking

Monitor your emotional state over time and gain valuable insights into your wellbeing.

### 🧘 Stress Assessment

Evaluate stress levels and identify potential emotional triggers.

### 💡 Emotional Insights

Receive feedback based on your emotional patterns and self-assessments.

### 🌬️ Guided Breathing Exercises

Practice breathing techniques designed to reduce stress and promote relaxation.

### 📈 Progress Monitoring

Track your emotional journey and celebrate personal growth.

### 📱 Responsive Design

Accessible across desktop and mobile devices for a seamless experience.

---

## 🎯 Mission

MindPulse aims to make emotional wellbeing more accessible by encouraging self-awareness, reflection, and healthy emotional habits through technology.
